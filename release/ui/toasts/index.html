toast-ui ⇒

toast-ui

Non-intrusive notifications that stack elegantly in any corner of the screen.

Toast Usage

⚠️ Only call renderStack() once in your app, or layouts, Toasts are a global component.

Toast Types

Four semantic toast types for different notification contexts.

Success

Confirm successful operations like saves, uploads, or completed actions.

Error

Alert users to failures, validation errors, or critical issues requiring attention.

Warning

Caution users about potential issues or actions that may have consequences.

Info

Provide helpful information, tips, or neutral status updates.

Stack Positions

Position your toast stack in any corner of the viewport.

Top Right (Default)

The most common position for notifications. Toasts stack downward.

Top Left

Alternative top position. Ideal for RTL layouts or left-aligned interfaces.

Bottom Right

Toasts emerge from the bottom and stack upward. Great for chat-style apps.

Bottom Left

Bottom left positioning with upward stacking. Pairs well with left-side navigation.

Stacking Behavior

Multiple toasts stack with smooth animations. Hover to expand and pause auto-dismiss.
